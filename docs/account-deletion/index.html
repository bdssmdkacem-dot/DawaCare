DawaCare — Account and Data Deletion

Developer: Abdessamad Kassim

This page explains how DawaCare users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

Send a deletion request through the DawaCare GitHub issue tracker:

Request account and data deletion

In the request, state that you want your DawaCare account and associated personal data deleted. Do not include your password or other authentication secrets.

Data that may be deleted

  • Account and profile information.
  • Medication and care-related records associated with the account.
  • Caregiver, patient and family relationship data associated with the account.
  • Messages and attachments associated with the account, where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained when required by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate technical record-keeping. Any retained information is limited to what is necessary and is handled according to the DawaCare Privacy Policy.

Processing

After verifying the request and account ownership, we will process the deletion request and remove eligible account-associated data. Some deletion operations may require reasonable processing time because data can be stored across application services.
